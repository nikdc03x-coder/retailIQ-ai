# Problem Statement

## The Problem
Retail sales records are useful but can be difficult
to understand without proper analysis.

## Questions We Want to Answer
1. Which stores generate the most sales?
2. How do sales change over time?
3. Can we forecast future sales?
4. Which sales periods are unusual?
5. What factors influence model predictions?

## Proposed Solution
A dashboard connecting clean data, business metrics,
evaluated ML models and explainable results.

## Important Limitation
Our system cannot prove why sales changed from
correlations or model explanations alone.
